2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the booking service.

a. Account information

When you sign in, we collect and store:

  • With Google Sign-In: your email address and basic profile information (such as your name) from your Google account, obtained through Google’s authentication. We do not receive your Google password.
  • With username/password sign-in: the username you use to authenticate.

b. Profile information

To complete your account, you provide:

  • Your full name
  • Your contact (phone) number

c. Location information

With your permission, the App accesses your device’s precise location while the App is in use (foreground only) to:

  • Show your current position on the map,
  • Set or suggest your pickup point,
  • Search for nearby places, and
  • Calculate routes between pickup and drop-off.

The App does not access your location in the background and does not track your location when the App is closed.

d. Booking information

When you make a booking, we collect:

  • Pickup and drop-off locations and addresses,
  • Pickup date and time,
  • Number of passengers, and
  • The status and history of your bookings.

e. Information stored on your device

Authentication tokens (used to keep you signed in) are stored securely on your device using the operating system’s secure storage. They remain on your device and are used only to authenticate your requests to our servers.

We do not knowingly collect payment card details, precise health data, contacts, photos, or biometric data through the App.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and manage your account and profile,
  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in,
  • Display maps, determine your location, and provide place search and routing,
  • Process and fulfill your cab bookings,
  • Communicate with you and the assigned driver about your trip, and
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your location or personal data for third-party advertising.

4. How we share your information

We share information only as needed to operate the service:

  • With drivers / cab operators fulfilling your booking: to complete your ride, the assigned driver or vendor is provided your name, contact number, and pickup/drop-off details.
  • With Google Maps Platform: location coordinates and search queries are sent to Google to provide maps, place search (autocomplete), geocoding, and directions. This is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • With Google Sign-In: used to authenticate you if you choose that option, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting/infrastructure providers: our backend servers that store your account and booking data on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the service.

We do not otherwise share your personal information with third parties.

5. Data storage and security

  • Data transmitted between the App and our servers is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Authentication tokens are held in your device’s secure storage.
  • We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
